# v0.6.0 — Block + Cooldown paradigm (Alejandro's rulings, 2026-10-26 14:42)

Replaces ejection with quarantine, cuts fade mode, and gates every block
behind two frames. Brainstorm rulings by Alejandro; build on his go only.

## Quarantine (replaces Home ejection in Enforce mode)
- On a confirmed block of package X: a full-screen cover goes up over X —
  attribution line + live countdown. Touches swallowed. NO Home press,
  no ejection, ever.
- Duration escalates per app: 20 s -> 40 s -> 60 s (cap 60). Escalation
  level resets after 30 min with no block in that app (proposed default).
- Countdown is wall-clock, never pauses. Leaving X hides the cover;
  returning with time left re-shows the remaining time.
- NO grading under the cover (Alejandro's explicit ruling). Brief flashes
  of content between cooldowns are accepted by design — the rhythm of
  block + growing cooldown is the friction.
- At expiry the cover lifts AND the diff baseline for X is reset, so the
  next capture is graded fresh immediately: a still-bad screen goes
  pending -> confirm -> re-block at the next escalation level. A static
  explicit image can never be "left alone" into indefinite viewing.
- Enforce OFF stays warn-only (message, no quarantine, no escalation).

## Two frames for every block
- Block-grade signals (detector >= 0.55, classifier FULL-frame >= 0.90)
  no longer block on sight: they become PENDING.
- Confirmation = one FRESHLY ANALYSED frame within 3 s at signal grade
  (detector >= user threshold, or classifier verdict >= 0.60). Static
  re-feeds NEVER confirm — a spike must not be able to confirm itself.
- While a block is pending for a package: cadence jumps to fast and the
  diff-skip is suspended for it, so the fresh second look lands in
  ~0.5-1 s even on a frozen screen. Clean confirm frame = pending dropped
  (logged as a dropped spike).
- Weak-only window pairs keep the v0.5.1 rules unchanged (partner-grade
  entry required). Incognito already needs 2 frames; unchanged.

## Fade mode is CUT (Alejandro's ruling)
- Remove the fade engine, its overlay, probation pre-charge, and the
  Settings toggles that only served it: "Instant block" (now the only
  mode), "Fast fade", "Flicker overlay off for captures" (it existed for
  the fade overlay's sake; the debug readout keeps hiding itself).
- Probation counters are repurposed as the quarantine escalation levels.

## Unchanged
- v0.5.1 corroboration (tiles weak-only; partner rule), classifier tiers,
  settings lock + unlock ritual, debug overlay, post-block watch (still
  useful for OTHER apps during a quarantine), no INTERNET permission.

## v0.6.1 field correction (Alejandro, 2026-10-26 16:06)
v0.6.0 as shipped trapped the user: the cover was a full-screen TOUCHABLE
window — it swallowed even the Home gesture (with a bad threshold that
would effectively brick the phone) — and it flapped on/off for ~4 s after
a block because showing it made the window poll report ScreenGuard's own
package, which the show/hide logic read as "user left". His correction:
- The block itself EJECTS to Home again (pre-v0.6.0 behaviour). The user
  lands on the launcher and can kill the app from recents.
- The timed cover exists ONLY for re-entry during the cooldown, and it
  must never trap: NOT_TOUCHABLE + NOT_FOCUSABLE, so every touch and
  system gesture passes through; the user can exit the covered app at
  any time. Its purpose is anti-exploit: a persistent user who reopens
  the app gets the timer, not the content, until the cooldown ends.
- Cover visibility is a state machine tolerant of own-package readings
  (the cover reading as foreground = the cover itself, not a departure).

## v0.7.0 thresholds redesign (Alejandro, 2026-10-26 17:21)
Decoupled conviction paths, after his field report: hardcore content
shows classifier fragment_max 90+ consistently yet never blocks (tiles
could not convict and the window partner — a detector hit — is starved
at his raised 0.70 detector threshold), while normal-content FPs were
detector-driven. Changes: (1) PERSISTENCE PATH, armed at his order (he
declined shadow mode): a tile-inclusive classifier verdict at/above
the scene threshold on 3 separate FRESH frames within 6 s convicts
alone. (2) A Scene sensitivity slider (0.50-0.99, default 0.90) governs
that path — the classifier finally has its own knob. (3) Linear penalty
restored: cooldowns 20/40/60 again. Unlock ritual stays at 10 s debug
values while thresholds are iterated. Accepted cost, told to him: a
sustained hot crop of suggestive content (cosplay family) can now block
via persistence; only the detector separates that from hardcore, and
it stays silent on both.

## v0.7.1 fine-tuning (Alejandro, 2026-10-26 19:40)
Battery pass, first instalment (his numbers): idle cadence relaxed to
5 s in risky apps / 15 s everywhere else — BUT any suspicious frame
(detector warmth 0.25+, classifier warmth 0.40+, any hit, a secure
frame) starts the MAX cadence (0.5 s) for a 3 s burst, renewing while
suspicion lasts; the burst also pulls the already-scheduled next
capture in, since analysis finishes after scheduling. Pending / weak /
persistence / secure-streak states keep their own fast cadence as
before. Cooldowns now DOUBLE: 20/40/80/160/320/600 s, then 600 s for
every further block; the 30 min clean reset is unchanged. Launcher and
Settings are exempt ABSOLUTELY (dynamic HOME-package resolution +
known-package fallback; no captures even during the post-block watch)
so the home screen can never be graded or locked.

## v0.8.0 configurability (Alejandro, 2026-10-26 19:44)
Folds in all of the unreleased v0.7.1 (cadence 5 s/15 s + suspicion
bursts, doubling cooldowns, launcher/Settings exempt). Triggered by a
field FALSE POSITIVE: a harmless spider reel in Instagram blocked via
classifier full-photo 0.90 plus a single 0.80 confirm frame (log
19:42:19-21) — so strong-path hits rise 2 -> 3 and weak-window hits

window, weak hits + window, persistence frames + window, secure

reset. Cooldown duration = base doubling per level capped at max
(quarDurationSec). Unlock OPEN WINDOW is 10 minutes at his order;
prayer + wait stay at 10 s debug values. The same log also showed the

frame dropped) and blocking com.oplus.screenshot — the launcher half
of that is fixed by the absolute exemption; the screenshot app is not
exempt (flagged to Alejandro, his call).

## v0.8.1 persistence witness (Alejandro, 2026-10-26 20:15)
The persistence path may no longer convict on fragments alone: at
least one frame inside the window must carry a backup witness —
detector warmth >= 0.35 or a non-trivial full frame >= 0.30
(PERSIST_WITNESS_DET / PERSIST_WITNESS_FULL). Held convictions log
PERSIST HELD. Attribution fixed: the reported score is now the max
of the actual convicting frames, not a session leftover (the ad
block read 0.99 from an earlier frame; its convicting frames were
0.94/0.95/0.96). Alejandro also opened the final-UI question: sliders
are a PoC tuning tool, and the app is leaving PoC — a presets +
plain-language UI is proposed for v0.9 (see chat 2026-10-26 20:15).

## v0.9.0 final UI (Alejandro, 2026-10-26 20:40)
Sliders are demoted from front door to calibration tool. Home now
leads with status, a Protection level (Gentle / Balanced / Strict)
and a live plain-language rules summary. The three presets are
CLOSE bundles (his ruling) — bars shift by 0.02-0.05, hits by at
most one frame, cadence and cooldown by small steps around the
tuned Balanced config; they are not different products. Every
control moved to a separate Custom fine-tuning screen, grouped by
path with a plain explanation per group; touching anything there
(or the two Home sensitivity sliders) marks the level Custom.
Verbosity and control are product values, not PoC scaffolding
(his note): the rules summary, the per-group explanations and the
full debug log all stay. Fixed safety rules (persistence witness,
weak partner, launcher/Settings exemption) are stated on the
Custom screen and are not adjustable.
